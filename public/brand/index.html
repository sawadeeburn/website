Give it room.

Leave clear space all round, as tall as the letter E in the name. Nothing else goes inside it.

  • Smallest size. The logo: 120 px or 25 mm wide. Smaller than that, use the W or the badge.
  • One mark at a time. The logo, the W or the badge. Not two together.
  • The name. Write it SaWadee Burn, with a capital W. Handles and links: sawadeeburn.

Please don't.

  • Stretch or squash it.

  • Use colours from outside the palette.

  • Outline it or add effects.

  • Tilt it. Stickers tilt. The logo sits straight.

  • Put it on a busy picture.

The look:Overprint.

A gig poster, printed in two inks on rough paper. Four things make it.

  1. Type that fills the frame. One short line, as big as it goes.
  2. Two inks. Where they cross, they mix. A little off register is fine.
  3. A round sticker. For the one extra fact: a date, a place. Tilt it.
  4. A tilted band. For one line of small text across the page.

A few rulesthat matter.

  • The W never burns. It is a wai, a greeting. No flame, spark or ember on it or near it. The same goes for the greeter.
  • Respect where we are. Keep the marks away from temples, Buddha images, monks and royal images.
  • No alcohol. Not beside the marks, not in the picture.
  • Nothing for sale. Not on things you sell, and not next to a sponsor. A burn has neither.
  • Say what we are not. This event is not an Official Burning Man event or in any way affiliated with Burning Man Project.
