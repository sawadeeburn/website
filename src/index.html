Built byeveryone who comes.

A burn is a gathering built by the people in it. There is nothing to watch, only things to join.

  • You make the program.Art, camps and music come from the participants.
  • Gifts, not sales.No vendors, no bartering, no sponsors.
  • Then it's gone.We pack everything out and leave the beach as it was.

Everyone bringssomething.

Bring yourself

First burn or fiftieth, you are welcome as you are. Bring a tent, enough for six days, and something to give.

Everyone needsa ticket.

In advance, only on Ticketmelon. One ticket covers all six days. We are a non-profit: ticket money pays for the burn itself.

Get your ticket on Ticketmelon
  • One per person. Artists and camp members too. The name must match your photo ID.
  • No tickets at the gate. And no day passes.
  • Only Ticketmelon. Anyone selling in a chat or a marketplace is mistaken or robbing you.
  • Refunds. All sales are final. There are no refunds unless the event is cancelled or postponed. If we don't sell enough tickets to make it happen, all payments are refunded, minus Ticketmelon's processing fee.
  • Transfers. The rules are not published yet. Ask ticketing@sawadeeburn.com before you buy.
  • Thai citizens and children. Write to ticketing@sawadeeburn.com.
