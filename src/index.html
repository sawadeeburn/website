Not a festival.

A burn is a gathering built by the people in it. There is nothing to watch, only things to join.

  • No lineup.Nobody is booked to entertain you.
  • Gifts, not sales.No vendors, no bartering, no sponsors. You share what you bring.
  • Then it's gone.We pack everything out and leave the beach as it was.

SaWadee Burn grew out of two small one-day burns in the jungle of Koh Phangan. This is the first full-length one.

Everyone bringssomething.

Art, a camp, music, a skill, a meal, a hand. Three ways in.

Bring yourself

First burn or fiftieth, you are welcome as you are. Come with a tent, enough for six days, and something to give.

Bring a camp

A camp is a home on the beach and a gift to everyone else: shade, tea, a workshop, a dance floor, a quiet corner. It has to offer something open to everyone. Just camping with friends? Free camping has plenty of space.

Every burn hasa welcome.

Ours is the wai: two palms pressed together. Sawadee means well-being. That is how we start the burn, with a welcome at the gate.

Look at the peak of the W in our name. That is the wai too.

We sell them in advance, only on Ticketmelon. The ticket covers all six days.

Get your ticket on Ticketmelon

We are a non-profit. Ticket money pays for the burn itself, not for anybody's margin.

  • Artists and camps too. Registering art or a camp is not a ticket. Each person needs their own.
  • One per person. The name on it must match your photo ID.
  • No tickets at the gate. And no day passes. Come late or leave early if you need to.
  • Only Ticketmelon. Anyone selling in a chat or a marketplace is mistaken or robbing you.
  • Refunds. All sales are final. There are no refunds unless the event is cancelled or postponed. If we don't sell enough tickets to make the event happen, all ticket payments will be refunded, minus Ticketmelon's processing fee.
  • Transfers and name changes. The rules are not published yet. If you need an answer before you buy, email ticketing@sawadeeburn.com.
  • Thai citizens and children. Write to ticketing@sawadeeburn.com about the Thai ticket or a child's ticket.
  • No VIPs. Nobody gets a special area or a list.

How we build

We are inspired by and plan to operate in keeping with the 10 Principles of Burning Man*. Larry Harvey wrote them. Here they are in our own words.

    {{PRINCIPLES}}

* The original text is on the Burning Man website. The principles belong to Burning Man Project.

Give only what'sappreciated.Take only what'soffered.

We are free to express ourselves. When what we do touches another person's experience, consent comes first.

Consent is freely given, informed, specific, clear, and can be taken back at any moment. Ask before you touch, join in, step into someone's space, hand over anything, or point a camera. Silence is not a yes. If it's not a clear yes, don't assume. Ask.

You don't have to deal with it alone.

The Safer Zone is a dedicated space at the burn for confidential support if you feel unsafe or your boundaries were crossed. You can also go to a Nomad, the Welfare team or anyone on the organising team. They listen and help. Nobody will blame you.
